# Sign in to Overleaf

**Sign in with browser** opens a separate window of Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium with its own profile. Sign in to Overleaf there as usual; the window closes once you are signed in.

- The session is stored in the editor's secret storage, and browser sign-in also keeps a copy in its private browser profile until **Sign out**. Neither is in your folder or settings.
- On a self-hosted Overleaf server, set `turnleaf.baseUrl` before signing in.
- No Chromium-based browser? Copy the `overleaf_session2` cookie from your browser's developer tools and use **Paste a session cookie**.

**Sign out** in the sidebar removes both copies again.
