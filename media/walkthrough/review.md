# Review comments and suggestions

Comments from Overleaf appear as markers in the gutter of your source files, suggested changes appear in the text, and both are listed in the **Review** tab of the Turnleaf sidebar.

- Reply, edit your own messages, resolve or reopen threads.
- Accept or reject suggestions.
- Select text and choose **New Comment on Selection** from the editor's context menu.

All of these are drafts until you Send.
